I am an **Assistant Professor (Research & Education) at the [School of Law, Durham University](https://www.durham.ac.uk/departments/academic/law/)**. I am an interdisciplinary academic working on the intersections of **emerging technologies, law, and cybersecurity**.

I bring together my background as a qualified lawyer and my PhD training in computer science to identify, and propose solutions to, novel legal and cybersecurity issues in emerging technologies such as digital assets and AI, including their exploitation by malicious actors or corporations. To do this, I use and develop mixed methodologies that integrate traditional and computational methods across law, criminology, and computer science. My primary aim is to advance evidence-based policymaking and research that creates a safer digital space and enhances agency for technology users.

Before joining Durham, I was a Lecturer at the [Department of Security and Crime Science, University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/security-crime-science/), where I led the Cybercrime modules. I completed my PhD at the [Department of Computer Science, University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/computer-science/), as a member of the [Information Security Research Group](https://sec.cs.ucl.ac.uk/). My thesis, *Cyber (In)Security at the Intersections of Cryptocurrency Regulation, Service Providers, and Users*, was supervised by Dr. Marie Vasek and Dr. Ingolf Becker. During my doctoral studies, I conducted research at the [Cambridge Cybercrime Centre](https://www.cambridgecybercrime.uk/), where I remain an active collaborator. I am currently an academic visitor at UCL Computer Science.

Since April 2026, I have been working as a consultant and editor for the Royal Society with Prof. Steven Murdoch. We are working as part of the Society's [Science and the Law programme](https://royalsociety.org/about-us/what-we-do/science-and-law/), specifically on a Primer for Electronic Evidence, which will guide judges and practitioners across the UK on the matter.

Research interests
------
* Cybersecurity risks of AI in research and legal practice
* Cybercrime detection and mitigation
* Darknet marketplaces
* FinTech/cryptocurrency regulation and governance
* Socio-technical risk analysis of financial AML frameworks
* Empirical and data-driven studies
* Computational law
