Law enforcement
------
The Metropolitan Police continues to use my paper and recommendations on cryptocurrency wrench attacks as a primary reference to inform stakeholders, the public, and other agencies.

Policy
------
* I was invited to present early findings from my study on cryptocurrency exchange terms and conditions to the EU's European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) and European Banking Authority (EBA) in a closed meeting.
* 2026: Legal Editor & Consultant for the Royal Society's [*Science and the Law – Electronic Evidence*](https://royalsociety.org/about-us/what-we-do/science-and-law/) programme, working with Prof. Steven Murdoch.
* 2018–2020: My project "Digital Law," which proposed amendments to Lebanon's Law No. 81/2018 on Electronic Transactions and Personal Data, placed 2nd in the IAAF Inter-University Competition. The amendments were considered in parliamentary discussions.
