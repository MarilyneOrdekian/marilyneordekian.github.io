Lecturer, University College London (2026)
------
**Cybercrime** (undergraduate and graduate, ~130 students). Module leader for UCL's undergraduate and graduate cybercrime course in the Department of Security and Crime Science. I designed and delivered interdisciplinary lectures and practical tutorials on cybercrime operations, the legal frameworks governing cybercrime, and the exploitation of financial systems. I was also a personal tutor and was responsible for assessment, marking, and supervising teaching assistants.

Regular guest lecturer, University College London (2022–2025)
------
* **Cryptocurrencies** (graduate, ~50 students). Lectures and assessment materials on the regulation and governance of digital assets and FinTech, focusing on the evolving EU and UK legal landscape, governance frameworks, regulatory compliance, anti-money laundering, and securities regulation.
* **Cybercrime** (undergraduate and graduate, ~140 students). Lectures and tutorials on cyberlaw and the legal dimensions of financial system exploitation, including criminal liability, regulatory accountability, and enforcement frameworks.

Supervision (2024–2026)
------
* **Data Empowerment Clinic, King's College London**: Supervisor on the project "Empowering the Looper Community," supervising four undergraduate and LLM law students on data stewardship and empowerment (with Prof. Sylvie Delacroix's team).
* **Master's dissertation supervisor, UCL**: Proposed and supervised "Using and Comparing LLMs to Analyse Legal Documents" (with Prof. Philipp Jovanovic).
* **Mentorship Programme, UCL**: Academic supervisor on "Understanding the Cybersecurity Practices and Customer Obligations of Banks in Europe." I designed and led a project in which law students carried out doctrinal and thematic qualitative analysis of bank contracts, examining financial institutions' cybersecurity obligations and consumer liability frameworks under European law.
